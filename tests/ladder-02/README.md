# Ladder 02: the senses the concept walk left uncovered (2026-11-19)

Question: how to say "the rest", "miss" (a target), "cause" as a verb, and an academic degree, and how to read / \ _ - aloud (roadmap "Open before v2" 4–5; outside review). Decided by the word-decision ladders (`docs/vocabulary-method.md` §0), not by argument.

Ladder 1, rung 4 (family-balanced lump rates from CLICS4 v1.0, Zenodo 19608447, kept outside the repo; `rates_rest.py`, `rates_cause.py`, adapted from `tests/balance-02/clics_rates.py`; "no copies" rows):

| pair | families lumping | rate | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTHER / REMAIN | 0 of 38 | 0.0% | two concepts |
| OTHER / REMAINS | 0 of 23 | 0.0% | two concepts |
| CAUSE / MAKE | 1 of 19 | 1.8% | two concepts |
| CAUSE / DO | 1 of 19 | 1.8% | two concepts |

CLICS has no REASON concept and no "miss = feel the absence of", so those pairs can't be measured; MISS (A TARGET) is a concept of its own in 57 families.

Ladder 2 (form): none of these senses is frequent enough in the concept list for a root of its own, so each is built from existing words (rung 3), or uses a root that already holds the concept:
- "the rest": **eni** "other" for the others; **gi wuto** "that remains" for what's left over (the two concepts never share a word).
- "cause" as a verb: **ukun** "reason, cause", used as a verb (content words have no fixed class, 4.2.3); `data/roles.tsv` now gives it an object. Not **tula** "make": CAUSE and MAKE are separate concepts in the world's languages. (Claude's earlier proposal, **tula**, was wrong by the method.)
- "miss" (a target): **su payon** "not hit".
- an academic degree: **gemo pi yesa** "level of learning".
- / \ _ - : built like 11.5's other signs, from ordinary words: **tenen** "line", **tenen panwe** "back line", **tenen fuso** "line below", **tenen eten** "short line" (minus stays **guti**).
