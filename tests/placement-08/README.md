# Placement 08: no word sounds like a shorter word plus a grammar word (2026-11-19)

Question: re-place the words that break rule J (design log 2026-11-19, outside review finding 1): stress falls on a word's first syllable and grammar words are unstressed, so a three-syllable word sounds like a two-syllable word followed by a grammar word (**tiwuye** "spring" ~ **tiwu ye** "some of the children").

Method: placement-07's `place.py` plus rule J (`j_bad3`, `j_keys2`) in every check; the start step keeps two-syllable roots first, so the three-syllable root moves, except where the three-syllable word is a loan (fixed while in design), when the two-syllable root moves. Also fixed: a filter inherited from placement-01 applied the full rules only to three-syllable candidates with a juncture-relevant first syllable (the accent and loan-vowel rules were safe anyway: `tools/accent.py` checks the whole lexicon). Grammar forms after the pronoun rerun (`tests/shortwords-04/runs/pron-k0.01-8.tsv`).

Result: 85 roots moved (`apply/map.tsv`): 78 rare three-syllable roots, and 7 roots inside a loan: school **ode → bote** (hotel **otelu**), different **bago → dala** (vaccine **bakusin**), tree **gala → toda** (garage **galasu**), again **moti → osin** (model **modelu**), spouse **ofin → toti** (office **ofisu**), matter **bidin → nuse** (video **bideyo**), butterfly **wanbo → bindi**. One clash was between two loans, balcony **balukon** ~ bar **balu** + **kon**: balcony takes the nearest rule-passing adaptation of its source, **balukoni** (0.172 against the world's forms, `tests/loans-adapted-02/score.py`; the old form 0.175). Rule J is now check 4 of `tools/juncture.py`; all checks pass.
