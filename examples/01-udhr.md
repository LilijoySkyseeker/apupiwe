# Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The United Nations adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948. It is the most translated document in the world, which makes it a good first long text for any language. This translation was made from the English and checked against the French, which is equally official.

Each paragraph is followed by the original English. Point at or tap an Apupiwe word to see what it means.

Some ideas in the declaration don't have a single word in Apupiwe yet, so they are described. Religion is **lite pi dakuwu** "the way of God", a court is **pu pi wabun** "a place of law", and the United Nations is **loso pi yuna la** "the group of countries".

The English text is from the United Nations, as published by the UDHR in Unicode project.

## pata pi sogan pi ewo we {.apu}

## diye pi pata {.apu}

ewo mesun pi yonyo pi ewo we ibe bawe pi ewo bo wan gun ewo. ewo we pon ibe sogan kige. dan su nebi yidu bun la se wan. mon fowo minwe, yinki pi enpa gun wun ewo we tayu win. ben pon gun yinki pi kidun, yen gun yinki pi bonwi.
{.apu}

> Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity and of the equal and inalienable rights of all members of the human family is the foundation of freedom, justice and peace in the world,

ewo la su dundi sogan pi ewo. wan la pon bebu bun la. bo win, wan la sin tula yefi ten yomo. bo ewo we loya yefi upe yen yefi yomo, yefi la win tula bonko wan la. ewo la ganin wun fowo minwe lewe upe. mon fowo win, dan oyi enpa, yen dan ibe gen pume feko fun enpa. mon bun, dan pon su nuta yen ibe tonya. ganin win gun ke tisan se we, yen ewo pa sin iko ben tun ewo we.
{.apu}

> Whereas disregard and contempt for human rights have resulted in barbarous acts which have outraged the conscience of mankind, and the advent of a world in which human beings shall enjoy freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and want has been proclaimed as the highest aspiration of the common people,

senya pi wabun kifa kuwi sogan pi ewo fan ewo la su kifa migi senya yomo pi no wungu. migi win gun lite fugi.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential, if man is not to be compelled to have recourse, as a last resort, to rebellion against tyranny and oppression, that human rights should be protected by the rule of law,

ewo we kifa yema fan yuna la lewe sonu pi nen nen.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential to promote the development of friendly relations between nations,

mon pata pi loso pi yuna la, ewo la pi loso win sin oyi moti wun son gewan sogan bawe pi ewo, yen son gewan bawe pi ewo, yen son gewan wun lowu yen futa ibe sogan kige. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we guge. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we nume ke upe. wan la pon sin waka yema fan ewo we ibe enpa ke fefa.
{.apu}

> Whereas the peoples of the United Nations have in the Charter reaffirmed their faith in fundamental human rights, in the dignity and worth of the human person and in the equal rights of men and women and have determined to promote social progress and better standards of life in larger freedom,

yuna la pi loso pi yuna la sin oyi "me din doku nan loso pi yuna la fan ewo we dundi sogan pi ewo yen enpa bawe. me din pon doku nan bun fan ewo we kuwi bun la."
{.apu}

> Whereas Member States have pledged themselves to achieve, in co‐operation with the United Nations, the promotion of universal respect for and observance of human rights and fundamental freedoms,

nade kige pi sogan la win gun ke bawe se we fan yuna la nebi tula minwe gen oyi ben.
{.apu}

> Whereas a common understanding of these rights and freedoms is of the greatest importance for the full realization of this pledge,

bo win, tonto pi loso pi yuna la iko pata pi sogan pi ewo we tun ewo we. ewo la we yen yuna we ibe pata win fun gen momu kige. ta ewo mesun yen loso mesun pi ewo we imu pata win lin nufi we. ta wan la walo nan wiwa fan dan ke dundi sogan la win. nan doku gi guge mon yuna pi nen, ta wan la pon walo fan ewo we tayu sogan la win. ta yuna la pon doku nan nen nen fan win. ta ewo we kuwi sogan la win fun feko mon yuna la pi loso pi yuna la. ta ewo we pon kuwi bun la mon fela gi yuna la win wungu.
{.apu}

> Now, therefore, The General Assembly Proclaims this Universal Declaration of Human Rights as a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations, to the end that every individual and every organ of society, keeping this Declaration constantly in mind, shall strive by teaching and education to promote respect for these rights and freedoms and by progressive measures, national and international, to secure their universal and effective recognition and observance, both among the peoples of Member States themselves and among the peoples of territories under their jurisdiction.

## wabun mena 1 {.apu}

ewo we diye nume nan enpa, yen wan la ibe bawe kige yen sogan kige. wan la nebi pume, yen nebi loya yefi upe yen yefi yomo. wan la kifa tula nen nen fun faba la.
{.apu}

> All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.

## wabun mena 2 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan we pi pata win. yefi fen kubo win. fisu pi ewo lu fisu pi unne lu lowu lu futa lu apu lu lite pi dakuwu lu yuna lu loso gi wan obu se, su kubo win. yefi pi wan lu diye nume pi wan lu yefi eni lu gen pume pi wungu lu gen pume eni, pon su kubo win.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration, without distinction of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or other status.

pi yuna pi wan lu fela pi wan, don bun gun enpa, lu yuna eni wungu bun, lu bun su wungu nen, lu bun ibe senya ke uyo fun lite eni, senya pi bun pon su kubo win.
{.apu}

> Furthermore, no distinction shall be made on the basis of the political, jurisdictional or international status of the country or territory to which a person belongs, whether it be independent, trust, non‐self‐governing or under any other limitation of sovereignty.

## wabun mena 3 {.apu}

nume yen enpa yen sonli gun sogan pi dan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to life, liberty and the security of person.

## wabun mena 4 {.apu}

dan su gun efon yonnu ewo fan doku su enpa. dan pon su gun efon ibe ewo, lu bika ewo, lu koda ewo. win gun feko fan fisu mesun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be held in slavery or servitude; slavery and the slave trade shall be prohibited in all their forms.

## wabun mena 5 {.apu}

dan su gun efon tula ten fupu dan nan momu. dan pon su gun efon tula dan ten yomo, lu tula dan su fun ewo, lu tula dan fun yefi. win gun feko yon fan fupu pi wabun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment.

## wabun mena 6 {.apu}

mon fela we, dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo fan wabun.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to recognition everywhere as a person before the law.

## wabun mena 7 {.apu}

ewo we gun kige fan wabun, yen wan la ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la fun kige. ewo we pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la fun kige se ewo gi su tula wan la fun kige fun pata win su efon. wan la pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan la se ewo gi walo fan ewo eni tula ben.
{.apu}

> All are equal before the law and are entitled without any discrimination to equal protection of the law. All are entitled to equal protection against any discrimination in violation of this Declaration and against any incitement to such discrimination.

## wabun mena 8 {.apu}

don ewo pa alun sogan bawe gi wabun eni lu wabun bawe pi yuna neke yi, yi ibe sogan fan pu pi wabun pi yuna min tula upe ben.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to an effective remedy by the competent national tribunals for acts violating the fundamental rights granted him by the constitution or by law.

## wabun mena 9 {.apu}

don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu dan, lu yonnu dan, lu tula kusa dan se yuna pi wan.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest, detention or exile.

## wabun mena 10 {.apu}

don pu pi wabun la waka fun sogan pi wan yen gen kifa pi wan, lu waka wun bi wan sin alun wabun, wan ibe sogan fan pu pi wabun la doma wan fun kidun. pu pi wabun la kifa doma wan lin ewo we nebi nagu. pu pi wabun la win kifa gun kige fan we, yen kifa gun enpa. win gun kige fan ewo we.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled in full equality to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal, in the determination of his rights and obligations and of any criminal charge against him.

## wabun mena 11 {.apu}

1\. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin alun wabun, wabun la kifa tula yi fun ewo gi su alun bun. win gun feko anko pu pi wabun la wuye wun yi sin alun bun. pu pi wabun la kifa wuye ben fun wabun mon doma gi ewo we nebi nagu. mon doma win, bun la kifa neke yi yefi we gi yi wupi fan kuwi nen.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone charged with a penal offence has the right to be presumed innocent until proved guilty according to law in a public trial at which he has had all the guarantees necessary for his defence.

2\. don dan sin tula yefi pa lu su tula yefi pa, yen wabun pi yuna efon ben mon nufi ben, yen wabun lifin pi yuna la pon efon ben mon nufi ben, pu pi wabun la kifa su oyi wun wan sin alun wabun. pu pi wabun la pon kifa su neke wan fupu ke gogo se fupu gi wabun efon mon nufi ben.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be held guilty of any penal offence on account of any act or omission which did not constitute a penal offence, under national or international law, at the time when it was committed. Nor shall a heavier penalty be imposed than the one that was applicable at the time the penal offence was committed.

## wabun mena 12 {.apu}

pi ewo mesun, don wabun denku su efon, ewo eni su gun efon inwa nume kunsu pi wan, lu inwa yonyo pi wan, lu inwa guyi pi wan, lu inwa gen pofi pi wan. ewo eni pon su gun efon alun dundi pi wan, lu alun dapo upe pi wan. dan ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi wan se ben.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his privacy, family, home or correspondence, nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation. Everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or attacks.

## wabun mena 13 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan kobi enpa mon bese pi yuna gi wan wuto mon. wan pon ibe sogan fan nume enpa mon bese pi bun.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of movement and residence within the borders of each State.

2\. dan ibe sogan fan kusa yuna mesun yen yon yuna pi nen. wan pon ibe sogan fan sigo tun yuna pi nen.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to leave any country, including his own, and to return to his country.

## wabun mena 14 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ditun pu sonli mon yuna eni, don ewo la fesun wan fan tula fupu wan. wan pon ibe sogan fan nume mon pu sonli win.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to seek and to enjoy in other countries asylum from persecution.

2\. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin alun wabun, yen ukun feko pi ben gun wun yi sin alun wabun, yen alun win su gun fun wungu, yi su nebi fone sogan win. don ewo pa oyi wun yi sin tula yefi pa, yen ukun feko pi ben gun wun yefi win ninsu gen momu pi loso pi yuna la, lu ninsu bansin bawe pi bun, yi pon su nebi fone sogan win.
{.apu}

> 2\. This right may not be invoked in the case of prosecutions genuinely arising from non‐political crimes or from acts contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 15 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo pi yuna pa.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a nationality.

2\. don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu sogan gi wan ibe fan gun ewo pi yuna pi nen, lu yidu sogan gi wan ibe fan lewe ewo pi yuna eni.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his nationality nor denied the right to change his nationality.

## wabun mena 16 {.apu}

1\. don lowu lu futa sin guge, wan ibe sogan fan bedan. wan pon ibe sogan fan diye yonyo. wan la ibe sogan la win nuwen bamo gi gun fun fisu pi ewo lu yuna lu lite pi dakuwu. lowu yen futa ibe sogan kige fan bedan. wan la pon ibe sogan kige lin bedan. wan la pon ibe sogan kige mon fugi pi bedan.
{.apu}

> 1\. Men and women of full age, without any limitation due to race, nationality or religion, have the right to marry and to found a family. They are entitled to equal rights as to marriage, during marriage and at its dissolution.

2\. ewo sa nebi bedan ga don wan la nen tayu enpa yen minwe.
{.apu}

> 2\. Marriage shall be entered into only with the free and full consent of the intending spouses.

3\. yonyo gun loso meli gi nuwo bo dan gun ewo. bun gun yinki pi ewo we, yen bun ibe sogan fan ewo we yen yuna kuwi bun.
{.apu}

> 3\. The family is the natural and fundamental group unit of society and is entitled to protection by society and the State.

## wabun mena 17 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ibe yefi nuwen ewo eni. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe yefi nan ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to own property alone as well as in association with others.

2\. don wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu yefi pi wan se wan.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his property.

## wabun mena 18 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan pume enpa. pi upe yen yomo, wan pon ibe sogan fan pume enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko fun enpa. sogan win pon gun sogan fan kubo lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko. wan pon ibe sogan fan wuye lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko fun enpa. wan nebi tula ben nuwen ewo eni. wan pon nebi tula ben nan ewo eni. wan nebi tula ben mon fela kunsu, lu tula ben mon fela su kunsu. wiwa yen tula yen dundi dakuwu yen kuwi gun lite gi dan wuye bun la nan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion; this right includes freedom to change his religion or belief, and freedom, either alone or in community with others and in public or private, to manifest his religion or belief in teaching, practice, worship and observance.

## wabun mena 19 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan ibe gen pume fun enpa, yen dan ibe sogan fan oyi enpa. sogan win pon gun sogan fan ewo eni su tula wan fun yomo bo wan pume yefi pa. pi bansin yen gen iko, sogan win pon gun wun wan nebi ditun bun la, yen nebi dunu bun la, yen nebi peton bun la. wan nebi tula win nan du iko we. lifin pi yuna la su bamo ben.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression; this right includes freedom to hold opinions without interference and to seek, receive and impart information and ideas through any media and regardless of frontiers.

## wabun mena 20 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan tonto enpa nan bonwi. wan pon ibe sogan fan senfu loso nan bonwi. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa loso fun enpa.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association.

2\. dan su kifa inwa loso. ewo eni su gun efon oyi wun wan kifa inwa loso pa.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one may be compelled to belong to an association.

## wabun mena 21 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan wungu yuna pi nen nan ewo eni. wan nen nebi tula win, lu no wungu gi dan waka enpa, nebi tula win fan wan.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to take part in the government of his country, directly or through freely chosen representatives.

2\. dan ibe sogan kige fan inwa doku gi yema ewo we pi yuna pi wan.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right of equal access to public service in his country.

3\. senya pi no wungu la kifa obu se gen momu pi ewo la pi yuna. ewo la kifa nebi wuye gen momu win nan waka feko pi no wungu la. waka win kifa nuwo moti moti. lin waka win, ewo we ibe sogan kige fan waka. dan kifa nebi waka kunsu, lu kifa nebi waka fun lite eni gi gun enpa fun ben.
{.apu}

> 3\. The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government; this will shall be expressed in periodic and genuine elections which shall be by universal and equal suffrage and shall be held by secret vote or by equivalent free voting procedures.

## wabun mena 22 {.apu}

dan gun ni pi ewo we, yen dan ibe sogan fan dunu yema se ewo we. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun nasi. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun ewo we. wan pon ibe sogan gi gun fun lite pi nume pi loso. wan wupi bun la fan kuwi bawe pi nen. wan pon wupi bun la fan guge enpa. yuna pi wan doku fan win, yen yuna la doku nan nen nen fan win. yuna mesun doku fan win fun yile pi nen yen yefi pi nen yen nasi pi nen.
{.apu}

> Everyone, as a member of society, has the right to social security and is entitled to realization, through national effort and international co‐operation and in accordance with the organization and resources of each State, of the economic, social and cultural rights indispensable for his dignity and the free development of his personality.

## wabun mena 23 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan doku. wan pon ibe sogan fan waka doku pi nen fun enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan doku gi gun kidun yen upe. wan pon ibe sogan fan yuna kuwi wan se su ibe doku.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to work, to free choice of employment, to just and favourable conditions of work and to protection against unemployment.

2\. don ewo la doku kige, wan la ibe sogan fan nasi kige. win gun kige fan we.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone, without any discrimination, has the right to equal pay for equal work.

3\. ewo gi doku, ibe sogan fan nasi gi gun kidun yen upe. wan yen yonyo pi wan kifa nebi nume nan bawe pi ewo nan nasi win. don wan wupi bun, ewo we yen yuna pon kifa yema wan fun lite eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Everyone who works has the right to just and favourable remuneration ensuring for himself and his family an existence worthy of human dignity, and supplemented, if necessary, by other means of social protection.

4\. dan ibe sogan fan senfu loso pi no doku la nan ewo eni. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa loso pi no doku la. wan nebi tula win fan kuwi upe pi nen.
{.apu}

> 4\. Everyone has the right to form and to join trade unions for the protection of his interests.

## wabun mena 24 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan wanan. wan pon ibe sogan fan ibe nufi enpa. win pon gun wun wan su kifa doku yiwo ten fefa. win pon gun wun wan ibe dili enpa nan nasi moti moti.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay.

## wabun mena 25 {.apu}

1\. dan yen yonyo pi wan ibe sogan fan ibe tonya fan nume upe. win pon gun wun wan la ibe tonya fan gawo upe. win pon gun wun wan la ibe pasu yen lonne yen guyi. win pon gun wun ewo we yen yuna yema wan la lin wan la simi. win pon gun wun ewo we yen yuna yema wan la lin wan la wupi bun. don wan su ibe doku, lu gun simi lu su nebi doku, lu gun pani lu ofin pi wan sin kono lu wan su ibe tonya fun lite eni gi wan su nebi kubo, wan pon ibe sogan fan gun yema se ewo we yen yuna.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a standard of living adequate for the health and well‐being of himself and of his family, including food, clothing, housing and medical care and necessary social services, and the right to security in the event of unemployment, sickness, disability, widowhood, old age or other lack of livelihood in circumstances beyond his control.

2\. mole yen tiwu ibe sogan fan gun kuwi fun ke fefa, yen gun yema fun ke fefa. pi tiwu we, yon don mole pi wan la yen sine pi wan la su gun ofin, wan la ibe sogan fan ewo we kuwi wan la fun kige.
{.apu}

> 2\. Motherhood and childhood are entitled to special care and assistance. All children, whether born in or out of wedlock, shall enjoy the same social protection.

## wabun mena 26 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan yesa. dan su kifa unfu fan konsin wiwa pi diye. tiwu we kifa dunu wiwa pi diye. ewo we kifa nebi dunu wiwa pi doku. ewo we pon kifa nebi inwa wiwa tisan fun nebi pi nen fun kige.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to education. Education shall be free, at least in the elementary and fundamental stages. Elementary education shall be compulsory. Technical and professional education shall be made generally available and higher education shall be equally accessible to all on the basis of merit.

2\. wiwa kifa yema fan ewo guge minwe. bun pon kifa yema fan dan ke dundi sogan pi ewo yen enpa bawe. bun kifa yema fan yuna we nade nen nen, yen gun sonu, yen efon wun eni pume eni. win pon gun feko fan loso we pi fisu pi ewo. win pon gun feko fan loso we pi lite pi dakuwu lu loso we pi gen pume feko. bun pon kifa yema doku pi loso pi yuna la fan kuwi bonwi.
{.apu}

> 2\. Education shall be directed to the full development of the human personality and to the strengthening of respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms. It shall promote understanding, tolerance and friendship among all nations, racial or religious groups, and shall further the activities of the United Nations for the maintenance of peace.

3\. mole yen sine ibe sogan fan waka fisu pi wiwa pi tiwu pi nen. waka pi wan la gun ke bawe se waka pi ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Parents have a prior right to choose the kind of education that shall be given to their children.

## wabun mena 27 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan inwa lite pi nume pi loso gi wan nume mon kiyo fun enpa. wan pon ibe sogan fan tana gen tula fan minen. wan pon ibe sogan fan inwa guge pi loya. wan pon ibe sogan fan dunu upe pi guge win.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right freely to participate in the cultural life of the community, to enjoy the arts and to share in scientific advancement and its benefits.

2\. don dan sin tula yefi pi loya lu gen tusi lu gen tula fan minen, wan ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi dapo pi wan mon bun. wan pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi nasi gi wan dunu se bun.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to the protection of the moral and material interests resulting from any scientific, literary or artistic production of which he is the author.

## wabun mena 28 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan yile gi sogan we pi pata win nebi lewe feko minwe mon. yile win gun yile pi ewo we, yen gun yile lifin pi yuna la.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to a social and international order in which the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration can be fully realized.

## wabun mena 29 {.apu}

1\. dan nebi guge enpa yen minwe ga mon loso. bo win, dan ibe gen kifa fan loso gi wan nume mon.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has duties to the community in which alone the free and full development of his personality is possible.

2\. lin dan fone sogan pi nen, ga wabun la gun efon tula ke uyo sogan pi wan. wabun la gun efon tula ben ga fan ukun sa. ukun ni gun wun dan tayu sogan pi ewo eni, yen dundi bun la. mon ewo we gi waka no wungu pi nen, ukun eni gun wun ewo we kuwi lite upe fun kidun, yen kuwi bonwi, yen kuwi upe pi ewo we.
{.apu}

> 2\. In the exercise of his rights and freedoms, everyone shall be subject only to such limitations as are determined by law solely for the purpose of securing due recognition and respect for the rights and freedoms of others and of meeting the just requirements of morality, public order and the general welfare in a democratic society.

3\. don dan fone sogan la win, yen ben ninsu gen momu pi loso pi yuna la, lu ninsu bansin bawe pi bun, dan su su gun efon tula ben.
{.apu}

> 3\. These rights and freedoms may in no case be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 30 {.apu}

fen pi pata win yofu wun yuna pa lu loso pa lu ewo pa ibe sogan fan tula yefi pa fan alun ni pi sogan pi pata win.
{.apu}

> Nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying for any State, group or person any right to engage in any activity or to perform any act aimed at the destruction of any of the rights and freedoms set forth herein.
