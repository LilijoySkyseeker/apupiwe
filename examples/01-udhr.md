# Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The United Nations adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 1948. It is the most translated document in the world, which makes it a good first long text for any language. This translation was made from the English and checked against the French, which is equally official.

Each paragraph is followed by the original English. Point at or tap an Apupiwe word to see what it means.

Some ideas in the declaration don't have a single word in Apupiwe yet, so they are described. Religion is **lite pi dakuwu** "the way of God", a court is **ban pi wabun** "a place of law", and the United Nations is **loso pi yuna la** "the group of countries".

The English text is from the United Nations, as published by the UDHR in Unicode project.

## pata pi sogan pi ewo we {.apu}

## diye pi pata {.apu}

ewo mesun pi yonyo pi ewo we ibe bawe pi ewo bo mon gun ewo. ewo we pon ibe sogan kige. dan su nebi yidu bun la don mon. no fowo minwe, yinki pi enpa gun me ewo we tayu yin. fin pon gun yinki pi kidun, nun gun yinki pi bonwi.
{.apu}

> Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity and of the equal and inalienable rights of all members of the human family is the foundation of freedom, justice and peace in the world,

ewo la su dundi sogan pi ewo. mon la pon bebu bun la. bo yin, mon la ben tula yefi ten yomo. bo ewo we loya yefi upe nun yefi yomo, yefi la yin tula bonko mon la. ewo la ganin me fowo minwe lewe upe. no fowo yin, dan oyi enpa, nun dan ibe gen pume feko fun enpa. no bun, dan pon su nuta nun ibe tonya. ganin yin gun ke tisan don we, nun ewo kin ben iko fin len ewo we.
{.apu}

> Whereas disregard and contempt for human rights have resulted in barbarous acts which have outraged the conscience of mankind, and the advent of a world in which human beings shall enjoy freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and want has been proclaimed as the highest aspiration of the common people,

senya pi wabun kifa kuwi sogan pi ewo fan ewo la su kifa migi senya yomo pi win wungu. migi yin gun lite fugi.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential, if man is not to be compelled to have recourse, as a last resort, to rebellion against tyranny and oppression, that human rights should be protected by the rule of law,

ewo we kifa yema fan yuna la lewe sonu pi nen nen.
{.apu}

> Whereas it is essential to promote the development of friendly relations between nations,

no pata pi loso pi yuna la, ewo la pi loso yin ben oyi moti me son gewan sogan bawe pi ewo, nun son gewan bawe pi ewo, nun son gewan me lowu nun futa ibe sogan kige. mon la pon ben waka yema fan ewo we guge. mon la pon ben waka yema fan ewo we nume ke upe. mon la pon ben waka yema fan ewo we ibe enpa ke fefa.
{.apu}

> Whereas the peoples of the United Nations have in the Charter reaffirmed their faith in fundamental human rights, in the dignity and worth of the human person and in the equal rights of men and women and have determined to promote social progress and better standards of life in larger freedom,

yuna la pi loso pi yuna la ben oyi "du din doku nan loso pi yuna la fan ewo we dundi sogan pi ewo nun enpa bawe. du din pon doku nan bun fan ewo we kuwi bun la."
{.apu}

> Whereas Member States have pledged themselves to achieve, in co‐operation with the United Nations, the promotion of universal respect for and observance of human rights and fundamental freedoms,

nade kige pi sogan la yin gun ke bawe don we fan yuna la nebi tula minwe gen oyi fin.
{.apu}

> Whereas a common understanding of these rights and freedoms is of the greatest importance for the full realization of this pledge,

bo yin, tonto pi loso pi yuna la iko pata pi sogan pi ewo we len ewo we. ewo la we nun yuna we ibe pata yin fun gen momu kige. lo ewo mesun nun loso mesun pi ewo we imu pata yin pu nufi we. lo mon la walo nan wiwa fan dan ke dundi sogan la yin. nan doku gi guge no yuna pi nen, lo mon la pon walo fan ewo we tayu sogan la yin. lo yuna la pon doku nan nen nen fan yin. lo ewo we kuwi sogan la yin fun feko no yuna la pi loso pi yuna la. lo ewo we pon kuwi bun la no fela gi yuna la yin wungu.
{.apu}

> Now, therefore, The General Assembly Proclaims this Universal Declaration of Human Rights as a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations, to the end that every individual and every organ of society, keeping this Declaration constantly in mind, shall strive by teaching and education to promote respect for these rights and freedoms and by progressive measures, national and international, to secure their universal and effective recognition and observance, both among the peoples of Member States themselves and among the peoples of territories under their jurisdiction.

## wabun mena 1 {.apu}

ewo we diye nume nan enpa, nun mon la ibe bawe kige nun sogan kige. mon la nebi pume, nun nebi loya yefi upe nun yefi yomo. mon la kifa tula nen nen fun faba la.
{.apu}

> All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.

## wabun mena 2 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan we pi pata yin. yefi pa kubo yin. fisu pi ewo lu fisu pi unne lu lowu lu futa lu apu lu lite pi dakuwu lu yuna lu loso gi mon obu don, su kubo yin. yefi pi mon lu diye nume pi mon lu yefi eni lu gen pume pi wungu lu gen pume eni, pon su kubo yin.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration, without distinction of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or other status.

pi yuna pi mon lu fela pi mon, sin bun gun enpa, lu yuna eni wungu bun, lu bun su wungu nen, lu bun ibe senya ke uyo fun lite eni, senya pi bun pon su kubo yin.
{.apu}

> Furthermore, no distinction shall be made on the basis of the political, jurisdictional or international status of the country or territory to which a person belongs, whether it be independent, trust, non‐self‐governing or under any other limitation of sovereignty.

## wabun mena 3 {.apu}

nume nun enpa nun sonli gun sogan pi dan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to life, liberty and the security of person.

## wabun mena 4 {.apu}

dan su gun efon yonnu ewo fan doku su enpa. dan pon su gun efon ibe ewo, lu bika ewo, lu bosen ewo. yin gun feko fan fisu mesun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be held in slavery or servitude; slavery and the slave trade shall be prohibited in all their forms.

## wabun mena 5 {.apu}

dan su gun efon tula ten fupu dan nan momu. dan pon su gun efon tula dan ten yomo, lu tula dan su fun ewo, lu tula dan fun yefi. yin gun feko yon fan fupu pi wabun.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to torture or to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment.

## wabun mena 6 {.apu}

no fela we, dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo fan wabun.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to recognition everywhere as a person before the law.

## wabun mena 7 {.apu}

ewo we gun kige fan wabun, nun mon la ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi mon la fun kige. ewo we pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi mon la fun kige don ewo gi su tula mon la fun kige fun pata yin su efon. mon la pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi mon la don ewo gi walo fan ewo eni tula fin.
{.apu}

> All are equal before the law and are entitled without any discrimination to equal protection of the law. All are entitled to equal protection against any discrimination in violation of this Declaration and against any incitement to such discrimination.

## wabun mena 8 {.apu}

sin ewo kin alun sogan bawe gi wabun eni lu wabun bawe pi yuna neke tun, tun ibe sogan fan ban pi wabun pi yuna min tula upe fin.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to an effective remedy by the competent national tribunals for acts violating the fundamental rights granted him by the constitution or by law.

## wabun mena 9 {.apu}

sin wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu dan, lu yonnu dan, lu tula kusa dan don yuna pi mon.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest, detention or exile.

## wabun mena 10 {.apu}

sin ban pi wabun la waka fun sogan pi mon nun gen kifa pi mon, lu waka me bi mon ben alun wabun, mon ibe sogan fan ban pi wabun la doma mon fun kidun. ban pi wabun la kifa doma mon pu ewo we nebi nagu. ban pi wabun la yin kifa gun kige fan we, nun kifa gun enpa. yin gun kige fan ewo we.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled in full equality to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal, in the determination of his rights and obligations and of any criminal charge against him.

## wabun mena 11 {.apu}

1\. sin ewo kin oyi me tun ben alun wabun, wabun la kifa tula tun fun ewo gi su alun bun. yin gun feko anko ban pi wabun la wuye me tun ben alun bun. ban pi wabun la kifa wuye fin fun wabun no doma gi ewo we nebi nagu. no doma yin, bun la kifa neke tun yefi we gi tun wupi fan kuwi nen.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone charged with a penal offence has the right to be presumed innocent until proved guilty according to law in a public trial at which he has had all the guarantees necessary for his defence.

2\. sin dan ben tula yefi kin lu su tula yefi kin, nun wabun pi yuna efon fin no nufi fin, nun wabun lifin pi yuna la pon efon fin no nufi fin, ban pi wabun la kifa su oyi me mon ben alun wabun. ban pi wabun la pon kifa su neke mon fupu ke gogo don fupu gi wabun efon no nufi fin.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be held guilty of any penal offence on account of any act or omission which did not constitute a penal offence, under national or international law, at the time when it was committed. Nor shall a heavier penalty be imposed than the one that was applicable at the time the penal offence was committed.

## wabun mena 12 {.apu}

pi ewo mesun, sin wabun denku su efon, ewo eni su gun efon inwa nume kunsu pi mon, lu inwa yonyo pi mon, lu inwa guyi pi mon, lu inwa gen pofi pi mon. ewo eni pon su gun efon alun dundi pi mon, lu alun dapo upe pi mon. dan ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi mon don fin.
{.apu}

> No one shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his privacy, family, home or correspondence, nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation. Everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or attacks.

## wabun mena 13 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan kobi enpa no bese pi yuna gi mon wuto no. mon pon ibe sogan fan nume enpa no bese pi bun.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of movement and residence within the borders of each State.

2\. dan ibe sogan fan kusa yuna mesun nun yon yuna pi nen. mon pon ibe sogan fan sigo len yuna pi nen.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to leave any country, including his own, and to return to his country.

## wabun mena 14 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ditun ban sonli no yuna eni, sin ewo la fesun mon fan tula fupu mon. mon pon ibe sogan fan nume no ban sonli yin.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to seek and to enjoy in other countries asylum from persecution.

2\. sin ewo kin oyi me tun ben alun wabun, nun ukun feko pi fin gun me tun ben alun wabun, nun alun yin su gun fun wungu, tun su nebi fone sogan yin. sin ewo kin oyi me tun ben tula yefi kin, nun ukun feko pi fin gun me yefi yin ninsu gen momu pi loso pi yuna la, lu ninsu bansin bawe pi bun, tun pon su nebi fone sogan yin.
{.apu}

> 2\. This right may not be invoked in the case of prosecutions genuinely arising from non‐political crimes or from acts contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 15 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan gun ewo pi yuna kin.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a nationality.

2\. sin wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu sogan gi mon ibe fan gun ewo pi yuna pi nen, lu yidu sogan gi mon ibe fan lewe ewo pi yuna eni.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his nationality nor denied the right to change his nationality.

## wabun mena 16 {.apu}

1\. sin lowu lu futa ben guge, mon ibe sogan fan bedan. mon pon ibe sogan fan diye yonyo. mon la ibe sogan la yin nuwen bamo gi gun fun fisu pi ewo lu yuna lu lite pi dakuwu. lowu nun futa ibe sogan kige fan bedan. mon la pon ibe sogan kige pu bedan. mon la pon ibe sogan kige no fugi pi bedan.
{.apu}

> 1\. Men and women of full age, without any limitation due to race, nationality or religion, have the right to marry and to found a family. They are entitled to equal rights as to marriage, during marriage and at its dissolution.

2\. ewo sa nebi bedan ga sin mon la nen tayu enpa nun minwe.
{.apu}

> 2\. Marriage shall be entered into only with the free and full consent of the intending spouses.

3\. yonyo gun loso meli gi nuwo bo dan gun ewo. bun gun yinki pi ewo we, nun bun ibe sogan fan ewo we nun yuna kuwi bun.
{.apu}

> 3\. The family is the natural and fundamental group unit of society and is entitled to protection by society and the State.

## wabun mena 17 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan ibe yefi nuwen ewo eni. mon pon ibe sogan fan ibe yefi nan ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to own property alone as well as in association with others.

2\. sin wabun denku su efon, dan su gun efon yidu yefi pi mon don mon.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one shall be arbitrarily deprived of his property.

## wabun mena 18 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan pume enpa. pi upe nun yomo, mon pon ibe sogan fan pume enpa. mon pon ibe sogan fan ibe lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko fun enpa. sogan yin pon gun sogan fan kubo lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko. mon pon ibe sogan fan wuye lite pi dakuwu lu gen pume feko fun enpa. mon nebi tula fin nuwen ewo eni. mon pon nebi tula fin nan ewo eni. mon nebi tula fin no fela kunsu, lu tula fin no fela su kunsu. wiwa nun tula nun dundi dakuwu nun kuwi gun lite gi dan wuye bun la nan.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion; this right includes freedom to change his religion or belief, and freedom, either alone or in community with others and in public or private, to manifest his religion or belief in teaching, practice, worship and observance.

## wabun mena 19 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan ibe gen pume fun enpa, nun dan ibe sogan fan oyi enpa. sogan yin pon gun sogan fan ewo eni su tula mon fun yomo bo mon pume yefi kin. pi bansin nun gen iko, sogan yin pon gun me mon nebi ditun bun la, nun nebi dunu bun la, nun nebi peton bun la. mon nebi tula yin nan wa iko we. lifin pi yuna la su bamo fin.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression; this right includes freedom to hold opinions without interference and to seek, receive and impart information and ideas through any media and regardless of frontiers.

## wabun mena 20 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan tonto enpa nan bonwi. mon pon ibe sogan fan senfu loso nan bonwi. mon pon ibe sogan fan inwa loso fun enpa.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to freedom of peaceful assembly and association.

2\. dan su kifa inwa loso. ewo eni su gun efon oyi me mon kifa inwa loso kin.
{.apu}

> 2\. No one may be compelled to belong to an association.

## wabun mena 21 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan wungu yuna pi nen nan ewo eni. mon nen nebi tula yin, lu win wungu gi dan waka enpa, nebi tula yin fan mon.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to take part in the government of his country, directly or through freely chosen representatives.

2\. dan ibe sogan kige fan inwa doku gi yema ewo we pi yuna pi mon.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right of equal access to public service in his country.

3\. senya pi win wungu la kifa obu don gen momu pi ewo la pi yuna. ewo la kifa nebi wuye gen momu yin nan waka feko pi win wungu la. waka yin kifa nuwo moti moti. pu waka yin, ewo we ibe sogan kige fan waka. dan kifa nebi waka kunsu, lu kifa nebi waka fun lite eni gi gun enpa fun fin.
{.apu}

> 3\. The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government; this will shall be expressed in periodic and genuine elections which shall be by universal and equal suffrage and shall be held by secret vote or by equivalent free voting procedures.

## wabun mena 22 {.apu}

dan gun ni pi ewo we, nun dan ibe sogan fan dunu yema don ewo we. mon pon ibe sogan gi gun fun nasi. mon pon ibe sogan gi gun fun ewo we. mon pon ibe sogan gi gun fun lite pi nume pi loso. mon wupi bun la fan kuwi bawe pi nen. mon pon wupi bun la fan guge enpa. yuna pi mon doku fan yin, nun yuna la doku nan nen nen fan yin. yuna mesun doku fan yin fun yile pi nen nun yefi pi nen nun nasi pi nen.
{.apu}

> Everyone, as a member of society, has the right to social security and is entitled to realization, through national effort and international co‐operation and in accordance with the organization and resources of each State, of the economic, social and cultural rights indispensable for his dignity and the free development of his personality.

## wabun mena 23 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan doku. mon pon ibe sogan fan waka doku pi nen fun enpa. mon pon ibe sogan fan doku gi gun kidun nun upe. mon pon ibe sogan fan yuna kuwi mon don su ibe doku.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to work, to free choice of employment, to just and favourable conditions of work and to protection against unemployment.

2\. sin ewo la doku kige, mon la ibe sogan fan nasi kige. yin gun kige fan we.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone, without any discrimination, has the right to equal pay for equal work.

3\. ewo gi doku, ibe sogan fan nasi gi gun kidun nun upe. mon nun yonyo pi mon kifa nebi nume nan bawe pi ewo nan nasi yin. sin mon wupi bun, ewo we nun yuna pon kifa yema mon fun lite eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Everyone who works has the right to just and favourable remuneration ensuring for himself and his family an existence worthy of human dignity, and supplemented, if necessary, by other means of social protection.

4\. dan ibe sogan fan senfu loso pi win doku la nan ewo eni. mon pon ibe sogan fan inwa loso pi win doku la. mon nebi tula yin fan kuwi upe pi nen.
{.apu}

> 4\. Everyone has the right to form and to join trade unions for the protection of his interests.

## wabun mena 24 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan wanan. mon pon ibe sogan fan ibe nufi enpa. yin pon gun me mon su kifa doku yiwo ten fefa. yin pon gun me mon ibe dili enpa nan nasi moti moti.
{.apu}

> Everyone has the right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay.

## wabun mena 25 {.apu}

1\. dan nun yonyo pi mon ibe sogan fan ibe tonya fan nume upe. yin pon gun me mon la ibe tonya fan gawo upe. yin pon gun me mon la ibe pasu nun lonne nun guyi. yin pon gun me ewo we nun yuna yema mon la pu mon la simi. yin pon gun me ewo we nun yuna yema mon la pu mon la wupi bun. sin mon su ibe doku, lu gun simi lu su nebi doku, lu gun pani lu ofin pi mon ben kono lu mon su ibe tonya fun lite eni gi mon su nebi kubo, mon pon ibe sogan fan gun yema don ewo we nun yuna.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to a standard of living adequate for the health and well‐being of himself and of his family, including food, clothing, housing and medical care and necessary social services, and the right to security in the event of unemployment, sickness, disability, widowhood, old age or other lack of livelihood in circumstances beyond his control.

2\. mole nun tiwu ibe sogan fan gun kuwi fun ke fefa, nun gun yema fun ke fefa. pi tiwu we, yon sin mole pi mon la nun sine pi mon la su gun ofin, mon la ibe sogan fan ewo we kuwi mon la fun kige.
{.apu}

> 2\. Motherhood and childhood are entitled to special care and assistance. All children, whether born in or out of wedlock, shall enjoy the same social protection.

## wabun mena 26 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan yesa. dan su kifa unfu fan konsin wiwa pi diye. tiwu we kifa dunu wiwa pi diye. ewo we kifa nebi dunu wiwa pi doku. ewo we pon kifa nebi inwa wiwa tisan fun nebi pi nen fun kige.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right to education. Education shall be free, at least in the elementary and fundamental stages. Elementary education shall be compulsory. Technical and professional education shall be made generally available and higher education shall be equally accessible to all on the basis of merit.

2\. wiwa kifa yema fan ewo guge minwe. bun pon kifa yema fan dan ke dundi sogan pi ewo nun enpa bawe. bun kifa yema fan yuna we nade nen nen, nun gun sonu, nun efon me eni pume eni. yin pon gun feko fan loso we pi fisu pi ewo. yin pon gun feko fan loso we pi lite pi dakuwu lu loso we pi gen pume feko. bun pon kifa yema doku pi loso pi yuna la fan kuwi bonwi.
{.apu}

> 2\. Education shall be directed to the full development of the human personality and to the strengthening of respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms. It shall promote understanding, tolerance and friendship among all nations, racial or religious groups, and shall further the activities of the United Nations for the maintenance of peace.

3\. mole nun sine ibe sogan fan waka fisu pi wiwa pi tiwu pi nen. waka pi mon la gun ke bawe don waka pi ewo eni.
{.apu}

> 3\. Parents have a prior right to choose the kind of education that shall be given to their children.

## wabun mena 27 {.apu}

1\. dan ibe sogan fan inwa lite pi nume pi loso gi mon nume no kiyo fun enpa. mon pon ibe sogan fan tana gen tula fan minen. mon pon ibe sogan fan inwa guge pi loya. mon pon ibe sogan fan dunu upe pi guge yin.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has the right freely to participate in the cultural life of the community, to enjoy the arts and to share in scientific advancement and its benefits.

2\. sin dan ben tula yefi pi loya lu gen tusi lu gen tula fan minen, mon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi dapo pi mon no bun. mon pon ibe sogan fan wabun la kuwi nasi gi mon dunu don bun.
{.apu}

> 2\. Everyone has the right to the protection of the moral and material interests resulting from any scientific, literary or artistic production of which he is the author.

## wabun mena 28 {.apu}

dan ibe sogan fan yile gi sogan we pi pata yin nebi lewe feko minwe no. yile yin gun yile pi ewo we, nun gun yile lifin pi yuna la.
{.apu}

> Everyone is entitled to a social and international order in which the rights and freedoms set forth in this Declaration can be fully realized.

## wabun mena 29 {.apu}

1\. dan nebi guge enpa nun minwe ga no loso. bo yin, dan ibe gen kifa fan loso gi mon nume no.
{.apu}

> 1\. Everyone has duties to the community in which alone the free and full development of his personality is possible.

2\. pu dan fone sogan pi nen, ga wabun la gun efon tula ke uyo sogan pi mon. wabun la gun efon tula fin ga fan ukun sa. ukun ni gun me dan tayu sogan pi ewo eni, nun dundi bun la. no ewo we gi waka win wungu pi nen, ukun eni gun me ewo we kuwi lite upe fun kidun, nun kuwi bonwi, nun kuwi upe pi ewo we.
{.apu}

> 2\. In the exercise of his rights and freedoms, everyone shall be subject only to such limitations as are determined by law solely for the purpose of securing due recognition and respect for the rights and freedoms of others and of meeting the just requirements of morality, public order and the general welfare in a democratic society.

3\. sin dan fone sogan la yin, nun fin ninsu gen momu pi loso pi yuna la, lu ninsu bansin bawe pi bun, dan su su gun efon tula fin.
{.apu}

> 3\. These rights and freedoms may in no case be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.

## wabun mena 30 {.apu}

pa pi pata yin yofu me yuna kin lu loso kin lu ewo kin ibe sogan fan tula yefi kin fan alun ni pi sogan pi pata yin.
{.apu}

> Nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying for any State, group or person any right to engage in any activity or to perform any act aimed at the destruction of any of the rights and freedoms set forth herein.
